FLIGHTBOUNTY Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

FLIGHTBOUNTY is a flying game for Android published by Phumigator. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, plane condition, level progress, music setting and ad choice are saved on your device. They stay there and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Top players table

When you finish the game, the name you type and the number of coins you paid are sent to our server at phumigator.online and shown publicly in the top players table. You choose the name, so please don't enter your real name or anything personal. The game doesn't ask for an account, email or phone number. This connection is not encrypted. Our web server may also keep standard technical logs, including IP addresses, for security and maintenance.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos through Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies. Videos play only when you choose to watch one. To deliver and measure ads, Unity may collect data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device details and how you interact with ads. Unity handles this data under its own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

At first launch the game asks whether the ad network may use your data to show ads suited to you. To change your choice, clear the game's data or reinstall it, and you'll be asked again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't use it for anything other than running the game and its top players table.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information in the table, contact us and we'll remove it.

Removing your data

To have an entry removed from the top players table, email us with the name and coin amount shown in it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
